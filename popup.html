⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

⌘+Shift+↑ (Mac)
Ctrl+Shift+↑ (Win)		 Toggle between current and previous tab
⌘+Shift+← (Mac)
Ctrl+Shift+← (Win)		 Navigate to older tab (by last accessed)
⌘+Shift+→ (Mac)
Ctrl+Shift+→ (Win)		 Navigate to newer tab (by last accessed)

🔎 Search & View Controls

Groups / All Groups shows tabs organized under colored group headers. All shows a flat sorted list with group badges.
▼ / ▶ button Collapse or expand all groups at once. Click individual group headers to collapse/expand one group. State is remembered between sessions.
✕ in search Clears the search text. Searching auto-expands any collapsed groups that contain matching tabs.
Any / All Any mode: clicking a chip deselects others (single-select). All mode: combine multiple chips with AND logic.

🎨 Border Colors (Tab Age)

🟢 Green ≤ 2 hours (recently accessed)
🟡 Yellow ≤ 24 hours (hours ago)
🟠 Orange ≤ 1 week (days old)
🔴 Red > 1 week (very old)
